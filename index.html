Trip Preparation

Packing & Weather

  • Layers are key: Tokyo is warm (~25°C), but the Alps are chilly (7-16°C). Bring a good jacket !
  • Walking shoes: Essential for Kamikochi and city exploring.
  • Rain gear: June is early rainy season. Pack a light umbrella or raincoat.

Pre-Trip Checklist

  • Internet: Buy an eSIM .
  • Transit Card: Buy a Suica Card.
  • Immigration: Fill out Visit Japan Web for faster entry.

Food to Try

  • Matsumoto: Shinshu Soba, Sanzoku-yaki (fried chicken), Wasabi snacks.
  • Tokyo: Fresh Sushi, rich Ramen, and Izakaya street food.
